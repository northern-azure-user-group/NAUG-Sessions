## About Us

**Join the North Azure User Group (NAUG)** – Community-driven cloud learning for the North of England.

📍 **Meetup:** [northern-azure-user-group](https://www.meetup.com/northern-azure-user-group)

### Our Mission

The aim of NAUG is to bring the IT community within Leeds, Manchester and surrounding areas together. The group is community-driven, delivering the type of content, speakers, and experiences that the IT community wants. We're open to developers, architects, consultants, engineers, and enthusiasts.

### What You'll Gain

- Learn best practices and optimal use of Microsoft's Cloud platform
- Get answers, advice, tips, and suggestions from peers
- Gain insights into what others are doing within Azure
- Network with local peers

## Community Event Playbook

### Speaker Pipeline

- **Call for Proposals**: Send CFP 4 weeks before event
- **Speaker Requirements**:
  - Title and abstract (200 words max)
  - Speaker bio and headshot
  - LinkedIn/social links
  - Tech/AV requirements
  - Consent to record and share recording
- **Slide Deadline**: 1 week before event

### Event Run of Show

**Typical format:**

| Time | Duration | Segment | Owner |
|------|----------|---------|-------|
| 18:00 | 30m | Arrival & Networking | Host |
| 18:30 | 5m | Welcome & Housekeeping | Host |
| 18:35 | 45m | Talk 1 | Speaker 1 |
| 19:20 | 30m | 🍕 Break & Networking | Host |
| 19:50 | 45m | Talk 2 | Speaker 2 |
| 20:35 | 5m | Wrap up & Q&A | Host |
