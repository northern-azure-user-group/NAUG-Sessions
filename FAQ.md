# Frequently Asked Questions

## Attending Events

### How do I register for a NAUG meetup?

Visit our Meetup page: https://www.meetup.com/northern-azure-user-group

### Where are events held?

Event locations are listed in the schedule in [README.md](README.md).

### Is there a cost to attend?

NAUG events are **free** to attend. All are welcome!

### What if I can't make it to an in-person event?

Currently, we focus on in-person events, so remote attendance is generally not available. You can still stay updated on topics, speakers, and any shared post-event materials through this repository and our Meetup page.

### What should I bring?

Just yourself! Bring a device if you'd like to make notes or follow along with demos. Networking is encouraged—meet fellow Azure enthusiasts!

## Speaking at NAUG

### How do I propose a talk?

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full process. In short:
- Email **hello@naug.co.uk** with your talk proposal
- Or reach out via our [Meetup page](https://www.meetup.com/northern-azure-user-group)
- Include title, abstract, bio, and availability
- Proposals reviewed 4 weeks before events

### What topics are we looking for?

We love talks about:
- Azure services and best practices
- Cloud architecture and design patterns
- DevOps and automation
- Security and governance
- Real-world case studies
- Tools and emerging technologies

Have an idea? Suggest it in an issue labeled `topic-request`!

### How long should my talk be?

Typically **45 minutes** with time for Q&A.

### Do I need to provide slides?

Yes, please submit slides (as PDF) **1 week before the event**. Feel free to share them in this repository after the event!
